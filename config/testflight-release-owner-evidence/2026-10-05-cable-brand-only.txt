### Objective

Simplify equipment selection for CABLE movements.

I do NOT care whether a cable station is:

- Plate Loaded Cable Station
- Selectorized Cable Station

Remove this secondary `Which version are you using?` step entirely for cable movements.

For cables, equipment identity should stop at:

CABLE MOVEMENT
→ select manufacturer/brand
→ done

Example:

Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown
→ Matrix
→ equipment selection complete

Do NOT ask whether the Matrix cable station is plate-loaded or selectorized.
