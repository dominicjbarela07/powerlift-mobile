# Strength Ledger atmospheric header assets

These images are non-semantic atmosphere layers for the premium contextual
header system. Semantic Squat, Bench Press, and Deadlift equipment must remain
separate and resolve through `lib/strength-ledger-visual-assets.ts`.

## `strength-header-v1.png`

- Mode: OpenAI built-in image generation
- Prompt direction: ultra-wide, near-black premium strength-training
  atmosphere; macro knurled bar and iron plate detail concentrated on the
  right; subtle violet light and powder texture; protected black title space
  on the left; no text, logo, people, or UI.
- Purpose: Strength Overview, lift detail, and strength-tier contextual identity.

## `achievements-header-v1.png`

- Mode: OpenAI built-in image generation
- Prompt direction: ultra-wide, near-black premium trophy-cabinet atmosphere;
  close metallic silver/gold/violet trophy detail concentrated on the right;
  restrained reflections and dust; protected black title space on the left;
  no text, logo, people, or UI.
- Purpose: Achievements Overview and achievement-family contextual identity.

## Permanent composition rules

- Semantic artwork is composed for its destination and is never arbitrarily
  cover-cropped.
- Contextual navigation is part of page visual identity and is never rendered
  as a generic SaaS subheader.
