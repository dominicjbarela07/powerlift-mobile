## Visual convergence

Inspected actual iPhoneAir/iOS26.2 output from the normal Expo Router app via
canonical npm-start/Expo Go, signed into user78/athlete68 (Athlete mode), against
localDEV backend5017 / strength_ledger_dev. This is the normal app Ledger and
dock, not an illustrative alternate entry. No production record was mutated.

The first pass was compared directly with the four retained concept images.
Three weaknesses were corrected before the second pass:

1. Header repetition and weak hierarchy: removed the redundant personal-record
   kicker, kept persistent destinations and applied coherent material panels.
2. Small values and ambiguous mini-chart meaning: enlarged performed values and
   captions, clarified90-day versus career context, labeled actual weekly/Session
   aggregation, and adapted preview widths for narrower phones.
3. Cramped/misleading muscle context: enlarged registered anatomy, excluded broad
   body regions from specific muscle bars, retained primary/supporting roles and
   labels, and added meaningful screen-reader evidence descriptions.

The permanent anatomy consumer guard rejected direct unregistered renderer
consumption during development; implementation was corrected to use the existing
ProgrammingMuscleRegionArt wrapper. Its protection was not changed. Actual
second-pass Records, History, lift detail and earned milestone views were
inspected. Source close retains the selected destination, filter and unit.
