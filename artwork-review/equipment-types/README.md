# Equipment-type art review

Equipment category art is separate from the canonical **movement** artwork queue,
which requires an exact movement definition ID. Do not register a physical
equipment category as a movement to bypass that queue's identity rules.

`plate-loaded-cable-station-v1` was approved by the owner for TestFlight on
2026-09-25, bound to the master SHA-256 recorded in its `review.json`.
Its master and 80 px preview remain available for inspection. This approval
does not change the separate Production artwork channel.

The selectorized cable station reuses the existing approved stack-and-pin
category asset. No new selectorized art was generated.
